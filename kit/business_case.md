# Business case template — one page per agent

**The problem in one number.** Last quarter at {site}, {KPI} averaged {x} against a target of {y}; the gap cost about {currency} {z} in {lost margin / penalties / expedite freight}.

**What the agent changes.** From {time from deviation to decision today} to {hours}; from {who finds it} to the planner's queue every Monday 07:00 with the cause, the money and the proposal.

**Value tree.** Service: {late cases avoided × contribution}. Inventory: {days of cover reduced × daily cost}. Freight: {expedites avoided × premium}. Planner time: {hours per week × weeks}.

**Cost.** {runs per week} × {cost per run} = {monthly tokens}; AWS {monthly}; one planner-week of setup.

**Risks and guards.** Wrong cause → propose-only and the approver; stale data → the data-stale check; runaway cost → the budget and the kill switch.

**The ask.** Six weeks, one site, one agent, these three people's time.
