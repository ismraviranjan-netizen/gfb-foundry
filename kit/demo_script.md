# The seven-minute demo, six beats

1. (0:00) The Monday screen: the monitor's list for week 12, worst first. "Chennai, mango nectar, OTIF 67% against 98% baseline."
2. (1:00) One click: the diagnosis. Two of three trucks from Nashik three days late, 1,762 cases slipped, Sahyadri Roadways. Kolkata holds 60 days of the same SKU.
3. (2:30) The money: ₹1.1 lakh at stake this week, stock at zero, P1 with a four-hour SLA.
4. (3:30) The plan: transfer from Kolkata on the four-day lane first, express carrier for the slipped cases, tell the customers. Each line names its approver.
5. (5:00) The approval: Priya approves the transfer; the audit log shows who, when, what. The agent never wrote to the ERP.
6. (6:00) The trap: Kolkata's own alert was a stale snapshot. The agent said "data problem, no action". Then the kill switch, live.

Interruption drill at minute three: "what if the carrier data is a day late?" → the snapshot-age check and the receipts cross-check, shown in the evidence.
