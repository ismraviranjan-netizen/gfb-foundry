# Discovery script — ten questions for a new company (60 minutes with the planning lead)

1. Which KPI does your boss read first on Monday morning, and what number was it last week?
2. When that KPI moves, who finds out first, how, and how long after it moved?
3. Walk me through the last exception that cost real money: what happened, who decided what, how long it took.
4. Which systems hold orders, stock, shipments and dock events, and how old is the stock number by the time a planner sees it?
5. Who is allowed to move stock between sites, change a carrier, change a dock schedule, change a production lot? Who must co-sign?
6. What is a case of your A item worth in contribution, and what does a late case cost you in penalties?
7. How many days of cover do you aim for at each site, and where do you run chronically short or long?
8. What does your busiest week look like against an ordinary one, and what is planned for it?
9. What has already been tried with automation or AI here, and why did it stall?
10. If this worked, what would the planner stop doing on Monday, and what would they do instead?

Write the answers straight into the profile file and the data-mapping sheet; if a question has no answer, that is a finding too.
